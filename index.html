Privacy Policy — Wen Salary

Last updated: 26 September 2026
Developer: CR DevTool
Contact: cr.devtool@gmail.com

The short version

Wen Salary reads bank alert messages on your phone to show you where your money goes. Everything stays on your phone. We do not have a server, we do not have an account system, and we never receive, see, store, or transmit your messages, transactions, balances, or any other financial information.

This is not a promise about how we behave. The app does not request internet access from Android at all, so it has no technical ability to send your data anywhere, whatever we might intend.

What Wen Salary reads, and why

Wen Salary requests the following permissions and no others:

SMS (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS). This is the core of the app. Wen Salary reads the transaction alerts your bank sends by SMS in order to detect the amount, merchant, card and date of each transaction, and record it locally so you can see your spending. Without this permission the app cannot perform its main function.

Wen Salary only processes messages that contain a currency amount and look like a bank transaction alert. Messages that do not match are ignored and never stored. Personal messages are not read, retained, uploaded, or shared. Wen Salary is not the default SMS app and does not send messages.

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). Optional. Used only to alert you when a category nears its budget or a new transaction is captured, and for the summaries you switch on: a daily summary, weekly and month recaps, and a reminder the evening before an expected bill. You choose these, and their times, in Settings → Notifications. Every figure in them is worked out on your phone.

Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED). Lets Android tell Wen Salary that the phone has restarted, so the reminders you switched on are set again. It reads nothing and sends nothing; it only re-books your own reminders.

Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT). Optional, and only used if you switch on the app lock. Android performs the check itself and tells Wen Salary only whether the unlock succeeded. Your fingerprint or face data is held by the operating system and is never made available to the app.

The app also declares one private permission of its own (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION). It is generated automatically by Android's own support libraries so that the app's internal broadcasts cannot be received by other apps. It grants no access to anything and is not visible to you.

Wen Salary does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage outside its own private folder.

Where your data lives

All transaction data is stored in a private database inside the app's own storage on your device. It is not accessible to other apps. It is never sent off the device.

We have deliberately switched off Android's automatic cloud backup for this app, so your transaction history is not copied to Google's servers or transferred to another phone without your action.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Wen Salary has no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking of any kind. It holds no internet permission, so it cannot contact any server, including ours.

What you control

  • Export / backup. You can save a copy of your data to a location you choose. Where that file goes is entirely your decision.
  • Deletion. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of its data. There is nothing held anywhere else, so there is nothing for you to request deletion of, and no account to close.
  • Permissions. You can revoke SMS access at any time in Android Settings. Wen Salary will keep working with whatever it has already recorded, and you can still add transactions by hand.

Children

Wen Salary is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

If a future version of Wen Salary adds paid features, payment would be handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google would process the payment and tell the app whether a valid purchase exists, and we would still never receive your name, card details, address, or payment information. That version would need internet access, and this policy would be updated to say so before it shipped. The current version has no paid features and no internet access.
